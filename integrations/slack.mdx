Waiting for a weekly report to discover a high-intent user or a churning account means your team is always reacting instead of acting. Connecting Sherlock to Slack puts engagement signals directly in front of the people who can act on them — sales, customer success, and growth — the moment a score crosses a threshold that matters. With the right alerts configured, your team stays proactive without ever leaving the tools they already live in.

## Prerequisites

- Admin access to the Slack workspace where you want to post alerts
- Admin access to your Sherlock workspace

## Connect Sherlock to Slack

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Slack integration in Sherlock">
    In your Sherlock dashboard, navigate to **Settings → Integrations → Slack**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Initiate the connection">
    Click **Connect Slack**. You'll be redirected to Slack's OAuth authorization page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authorize Sherlock in your Slack workspace">
    Review the permissions Sherlock requests, select the Slack workspace you want to connect, and click **Allow**. You'll be redirected back to Sherlock once authorization is complete.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a default notification channel">
    From the **Default Channel** dropdown, select the Slack channel where Sherlock should post alerts. You can override this per alert type after saving.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save your settings">
    Click **Save**. Sherlock will post a confirmation message to your chosen channel to verify the connection is working.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Create a dedicated **#sherlock-alerts** channel before connecting. Routing all Sherlock notifications to a single, purpose-built channel makes it easy for the whole team to monitor engagement signals without cluttering general or team-specific channels.
</Tip>

## Alert Types

Sherlock supports three categories of Slack alert. Each can be enabled or disabled independently and routed to a different channel.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="PQL Alerts">
    **Product Qualified Lead (PQL) Alerts** fire when a user's or account's engagement score crosses the PQL threshold you've defined in Sherlock's scoring settings.

    The alert message includes:
    - User or account name
    - Current engagement score
    - The events that drove the score over the threshold
    - A direct link to the user or account profile in Sherlock

    PQL alerts are ideal for routing to a sales or SDR channel so reps can follow up while intent is high.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Churn Risk Alerts">
    **Churn Risk Alerts** fire when a user's or account's engagement score drops below your configured churn-risk threshold. A declining score can signal disengagement before the customer ever contacts support or submits a cancellation request.

    The alert message includes:
    - User or account name
    - Current score and the score at peak engagement
    - Last active date
    - A direct link to the profile in Sherlock

    Route churn risk alerts to your customer success channel so CSMs can reach out proactively.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Score Milestones">
    **Score Milestone Alerts** fire when a user reaches a new personal high score. Milestones signal deepening product adoption and are useful for identifying expansion opportunities or candidates for case studies and advocacy programs.

    The alert message includes:
    - User name and associated account
    - New high score
    - Top contributing events

    Consider routing milestone alerts to a shared wins channel to celebrate product adoption across the team.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Customizing Alert Thresholds

Each alert type has its own threshold setting, letting you tune sensitivity independently:

- **PQL threshold** — Set the minimum engagement score a user or account must reach to trigger a PQL alert. Navigate to **Settings → Scoring → PQL Threshold** to adjust this value.
- **Churn risk threshold** — Set the maximum engagement score below which Sherlock considers a user or account at risk. Navigate to **Settings → Scoring → Churn Risk Threshold**.
- **Score milestone cadence** — Choose whether milestone alerts fire on every new high score or only at defined intervals (e.g., every 10-point increase).

You can also override the **destination channel** for each alert type from the Slack integration settings page, so PQL alerts go to `#sales-pipeline` while churn alerts go to `#cs-risk`.

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="I'm not receiving alerts in Slack">
    - Confirm that the alert type you expect is toggled **Enabled** in **Settings → Integrations → Slack**.
    - Check that the destination channel is spelled correctly and that Sherlock's bot has been **invited to the channel** (run `/invite @Sherlock` in the channel if needed).
    - Verify that your scoring thresholds are set to realistic values — if the PQL threshold is set very high, no alerts will fire until scores reach that level.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The Sherlock bot was posting but has stopped">
    Slack OAuth tokens can expire or be revoked if a Slack admin removes the app or rotates credentials. Navigate to **Settings → Integrations → Slack** and click **Re-authorize** to generate a fresh token and restore posting.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
