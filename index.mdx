Sherlock transforms your raw product usage data into a clear engagement score for every user and account on your platform. By weighting the events that matter most to your product, Sherlock gives you a definitive ranking of your most and least engaged customers — so your sales, success, and product teams always know exactly where to focus.

## How Sherlock Works

Sherlock is built on three simple ideas: capture every in-app action, let you decide what matters, and surface the results everywhere your team works.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect your data source">
    Link your [Segment](https://segment.com) workspace to Sherlock. All product events you already track begin flowing in immediately — no extra instrumentation required.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Weight your events">
    Assign a weight from 1–10 to each event based on how much it signals true engagement in your product. A user completing onboarding might score a 9; a page view might score a 1.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Let Sherlock score your users and accounts">
    Sherlock automatically calculates engagement scores for every user and account and keeps them up to date as new events arrive.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Act on the scores">
    Surface scores in Slack, push them to your CRM, or pull them via the API to drive PQL workflows, churn alerts, and customer health dashboards.
  </Step>
</Steps>
