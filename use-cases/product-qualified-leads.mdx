A Product Qualified Lead (PQL) is a user or account that has experienced genuine value in your product — not just clicked an ad or downloaded a whitepaper. For product-led growth companies, PQLs are a far more reliable signal than Marketing Qualified Leads (MQLs) because they're rooted in actual behavior: the prospect has already done something meaningful inside your product. Sherlock turns that behavior into a score, and when the score crosses your defined threshold, it automatically surfaces the lead to your sales team at exactly the right moment.

## How Sherlock Identifies PQLs

Sherlock continuously calculates engagement scores for every user and account by summing the weighted values of the product events they fire. You define what "meaningful engagement" looks like by setting a score threshold and a time window. Any user or account whose score meets or exceeds that threshold within the window is automatically flagged as a PQL — no manual review required.

For example, you might decide that a score of **60 or higher in the last 30 days** represents a lead worth pursuing. Sherlock monitors every account against that bar in real time and raises the PQL flag the moment an account crosses it.

## Configure Your PQL Threshold

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open PQL Settings">
    In the Sherlock sidebar, navigate to **Settings → PQL Settings**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set your score threshold">
    Enter the minimum engagement score a user or account must reach to qualify as a PQL. A common starting point is **60**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set your time window">
    Choose the rolling time window over which the score is evaluated — for example, **30 days**. Only events fired within this window count toward the PQL calculation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save your configuration">
    Click **Save**. Sherlock immediately begins evaluating all users and accounts against your new threshold.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Viewing PQL-Flagged Users and Accounts

Once your threshold is saved, any user or account that qualifies appears with a **PQL badge** in both the **Users** tab and the **Accounts** tab. You can filter either view to show only PQL-flagged records, making it easy for your sales team to build a prioritized outreach list.

## Push PQLs to Your CRM

Sherlock automatically syncs a `pql_status: true` field to the corresponding contact and company records in your connected CRM whenever a user or account crosses the PQL threshold. Your sales reps can create CRM views, triggers, and sequences based on that field — so the moment a lead qualifies, it lands in the right workflow without any manual handoff.

## Get Slack Alerts for New PQLs

Don't make your sales team refresh a dashboard. Instead, send them an instant notification the moment a new PQL emerges.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Notification Settings">
    Navigate to **Settings → Notifications**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable PQL Alert">
    Toggle on **PQL Alert** and select the Slack channel where alerts should be posted.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save**. Your team will now receive a Slack message every time an account crosses the PQL threshold, including the account name, current score, and a direct link to the record in Sherlock.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Real-World Example

> A free trial account has three active users. Over the past two weeks, they have collectively completed onboarding, connected a third-party integration, and exported their first data set. Each of those actions carries a high event weight in Sherlock, pushing the account's score to 74 — well above the configured threshold of 60. Sherlock immediately flags the account as a PQL and posts an alert to the `#sales-pql` Slack channel. The assigned sales rep sees the notification, opens the Sherlock record, reviews the engagement timeline, and reaches out with a personalized message referencing exactly what the team has already accomplished in the product.

<Tip>
  Start with a **conservative threshold** (higher score, e.g., 70–80) when you first go live. This keeps your initial PQL list small and high-confidence, making it easy to validate that flagged accounts genuinely convert. Once you've confirmed the signal, tune the threshold down gradually to widen the funnel.
</Tip>
